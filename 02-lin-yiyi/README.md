Q-version avatar of Lin Yiyi （林一一）, the character from the Douyin drama 《我的妹妹不可爱》 ("My Sister Is Not Cute"). Pixel-art chibi style, drawn from her look in the drama, redrawn as a big-head chibi: long dark brown twin tails tied with black bows, large black eyes, pink cheek blush, cream dress with a black collar and bow, white socks and black shoes.
