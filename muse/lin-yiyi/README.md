# lin-yiyi/ — Q-version Lin Yiyi avatar

**English | [中文](README.zh-CN.md)**

Q-version avatar of Lin Yiyi （林一一）, the character from the Douyin drama
《我的妹妹不可爱》 ("My Sister Is Not Cute"). Pixel-art chibi style, drawn from
her look in the drama — long dark hair, white top with a black ribbon — redrawn
as a big-head chibi: long dark brown twin tails tied with black bows, large
black eyes, pink cheek blush, cream dress with a black collar and bow, white
socks and black shoes.

It became the active avatar of the Muse personal assistant on 2026-11-20,
replacing the Stitch avatar.
