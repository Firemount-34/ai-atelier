# 01 · Hanfu & Qipao Series — 汉服旗袍系列

**English | [中文](README.zh-CN.md)**

The traditional-Chinese-style looks of the wardrobe, grouped as one series:
four hanfu outfits plus one qipao, all worn by the same big-head chibi
character in her signature typing-on-a-laptop pose. Four of them are part of
the weekly avatar rotation (Mon / Tue / Thu / Sat); the qipao is the spare.
